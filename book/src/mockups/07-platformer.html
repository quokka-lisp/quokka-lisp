Use case 4 · A game, changed while it runs

A small platform game with graphics and sound. The game loop is a process; clicking a drawing pauses it and inspects the real object; a changed method takes effect on the next frame.

A small platformer in Quokka: running in a viewer, paused and inspected by clicking the player, changed while running, and the rejected app mode.
