Environment mock-ups

Drawn on 27 Nov 2026 to work out how a person uses Quokka once phases 4 to 6 exist. They were made to decide the design, recorded in docs/design.md under Environment and UI (decisions #126 and #21), and nothing in them is built. Where the design moved on after a mock-up was drawn, its caption says so.

  1. Follow a book chapter · use case 1: a typo stops and asks, a restart resumes
  2. The listener as live text · use case 1: an edited form runs again at the bottom
  3. Find where something lives · use case 2: from the prompt to the code that drew it
  4. Browse, inspect, edit, commit · use case 2: the world as a database of definitions
  5. Package-first development · systems, packages, a package as text
  6. Build a small application · use case 3: the Rolodex, changed while it runs
  7. A game, changed while it runs · use case 4: processes, drawings as presentations, the rejected app mode
  8. Save and come back · use case 5: no Save button
  9. Change the system itself · use case 6: try a compiler change in a copy of the world
  10. Three window models · #21: tracks, columns, or a tree of splits

Each page has buttons for its steps and opens at #step=N on step N. A PNG of every step is in png/ for pages and print, made by scripts/render-mockups.sh; rerun it after changing a page. Colours and fonts are placeholders, not Quokka's theme.
